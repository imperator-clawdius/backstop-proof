# Terms of Service Template

This template is not legal advice and requires legal review before public use.

## Service

Backstop Proof provides merchant-controlled tools for packing proof capture, hash-sealed file records, audit trails, evidence pack generation, and dispute evidence draft preparation.

## Merchant Responsibility

Merchants are responsible for reviewing all evidence, rebuttal text, policies, and customer communications before using them in a dispute response.

## No Legal Advice

Backstop Proof does not provide legal advice, legal representation, or managed chargeback services.

## No Guaranteed Outcomes

Backstop Proof does not guarantee dispute reversals, issuer decisions, card network decisions, or recovery amounts.

## Billing

Public Shopify App Store distribution must use Shopify App Pricing/Billing API. Off-platform payment processing is not part of the MVP.

## Data Handling

The app processes Shopify order, customer, fulfillment, proof, dispute, audit, settings, and notification data only as needed to provide the service.

## Uninstall

On uninstall, Backstop Proof receives Shopify's app uninstall webhook and deletes associated shop records and app data.
